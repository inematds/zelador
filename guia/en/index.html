In short

If you leave bots and AI routines running on their own, you only find out about a problem when someone complains. Zelador is a free agent, a program that works on your computer and keeps an eye on things for you: every night, it checks what went wrong on your computer, and every week it gathers what is worth fixing in a simple form for you to decide. How it works

A building caretaker, in plain language

Think of a building caretaker: they make their rounds at night, write down what they find, then ask the building manager what should be fixed.

🔦 1. The night rounds

Every night, it reads your computer’s records—the notes showing what each routine did—and sorts out what is serious from what just needs attention. By looking at the computer’s history, it learns each routine’s usual schedule and notices when one misses its time. Then it sends you a summary.

📋 2. The weekly list

Every Monday, it gathers recurring errors, bad nights, things that got in your way, and forgotten tasks, with a priority for each one.

✍️ 3. Your decision

With AI explanations turned on, each item says what happened, the likely cause, and a suggested fix. Without it, you get a ready-made summary. You decide what to do.

🧩 It adapts to what you have

Don’t keep notes yet? It creates four simple lists: what went wrong, what you learned, what got in your way, and what you left for later.

Already keep notes under other names? If you don’t have its lists yet, it uses yours without making copies, and your AI checks whether the format works.

Already have other alerts? It suggests using what they produce.

🔒 What it doesn’t do

It doesn’t fix anything on its own: it finds issues, explains them, and suggests fixes.

It doesn’t overwrite your files.

It doesn’t send your data to a service of its own, because it doesn’t have one. The only things that leave your computer are what you turn on: the notice and AI explanations.

Before you install

Is it right for you?

It’s best to know now what it can and can’t see.

✅ It’s a good fit if

  • Your routines run on a computer with Linux, an operating system like Windows that is often used on computers that stay on, and they were scheduled using the same user account you use to log in. Not sure? Your AI can check before installing.
  • This computer usually stays on. If it’s off during the night rounds, it checks when the computer is back on. If the computer restarts, or you log out of your account, it may stop until a one-time adjustment is made with the administrator password. Your AI’s initial check will tell you about this and whether anything is missing.
  • You use Claude Code or Codex. They install it and check whether your computer has what it needs.

🚫 What it can’t see

  • Things that run on internet services, like a scheduled post on social media.
  • Routines belonging to the administrator or another computer user.
  • A scheduled task that doesn’t save a record—a file where it notes what it did. In that case, the notice says it couldn’t check.
  • An error the task doesn’t write to its record, or writes in words it doesn’t recognize. Ask your AI to check each routine during installation.
  • Routines that run in the cloud, even those created by your AI.
  • A wrong result that leaves no sign, unless the failure is written down.

📱 What about a phone or another computer?

You get the notice wherever you choose. The form opens only on the computer where Zelador is installed. To open it on a phone or another computer, you need a private network, a secure connection between your devices. Your AI can guide you.

💻 Mac and Windows

We haven’t tested it yet. Outside Linux, it would be able to see much less, so don’t count on it working with these systems for now.

🔕 What if no message arrives?

If you turned on the notice, check what’s wrong. It sends a line even on quiet nights, so silence means something stopped the rounds. If that happens, ask your AI to check.

Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste the request, read the plan, and say yes or no. Your AI installs it.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Zelador is free. You just need to already have Claude Code or Codex. AI explanations are optional and use a little of your AI allowance.

How do I get the notice?

You choose during installation. Telegram works best: it waits for you in the morning. You’ll need to create a bot there, and your AI can guide you. You can also use an on-screen notification, which appears overnight and may be easy to miss, or just save the report.

What if I already write everything down?

It never overwrites your files. The initial check shows what it can use and what would need adjusting, and nothing changes until you say yes.

Does my data leave my computer?

Zelador doesn’t have any internet service of its own. Only the things you turn on leave your computer, but keep in mind: the notice may include parts of the records, and the explanation sends your AI the details for each item and the end of the records. It tries to hide passwords, but can’t guarantee it catches them all. If the records include client or patient data, ask your AI to remove those parts from the notice and leave explanations turned off.

Does it fix errors by itself?

No, by design. It gives you a suggestion, and you decide. Then just ask your AI to do what you approved.
