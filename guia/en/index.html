In short

When you leave robots and AI routines running on their own, you only find out about a problem when someone complains. Zelador is a free agent, a program that works on its own on your computer and keeps an eye on this for you: every night it checks what went wrong on your computer, and every week it gathers what is worth improving in a simple form for you to decide. If you don't keep track of what goes wrong yet, it creates the lists. If you already do, it uses yours. To install it, just ask your AI.

How it works

A building caretaker, without the jargon

Think of a building caretaker: they make their rounds at night, write down what they find, and then ask the building manager what should be fixed.

🔦 1. The night round

Every night it reads your computer's records—the notes about what each routine did—and separates what is serious from what just needs attention. From the computer's history, it learns each routine's usual schedule and notices when one is late. Then it sends the summary.

📋 2. The week's agenda

Every Monday it gathers recurring errors, bad nights, things that blocked you, and forgotten tasks, with a priority for each one.

✍️ 3. Your decision

With your AI turned on, each item explains what happened, the likely cause, and a suggested fix. Without it, you get a ready-to-use summary. You decide.

🧩 It adapts to what you have

Don't keep any notes yet? It creates four simple lists: what went wrong, what you learned, what blocked you, and what was left for later.

Already keep notes under other names? If you don't have its lists yet, it uses yours without making a copy, and your AI checks whether the format works.

Already have other alerts? It suggests using what they produce.

🔒 What it doesn't do

It doesn't fix anything on its own: it finds, explains, and suggests.

It doesn't overwrite your files.

It doesn't send your data to a service of its own, because there isn't one. Only what you enable leaves your computer: the notice and the explanation through your AI.

Before you install

Is it right for you?

It's best to know now what it can and can't see.

✅ It works for you if

  • Your routines run on a computer with Linux, an operating system like Windows that's widely used on computers that stay on all the time, and were scheduled under the same user account you use to sign in. Not sure? Your AI can check before installing.
  • This computer usually stays on. If it's off during the round, it makes the round when it comes back on. If the computer restarts, or you sign out of your account, it may stop until a one-time adjustment is made with the administrator password. Your AI's initial check will warn you about this and tell you if any component is missing.
  • You use Claude Code or Codex. They install it and check whether your computer has what it needs.

🚫 What it can't see

  • What runs on internet services, like a post scheduled within a social network.
  • Routines run by the administrator or another user on the computer.
  • A scheduled task that doesn't keep a record—a file where it notes what it did. In that case, the notice says it couldn't check.
  • An error the task doesn't write to its record, or writes using words it doesn't recognize. Ask your AI to check each routine during installation.
  • Routines that run in the cloud, even ones created by your AI.
  • A wrong result that leaves no sign, unless someone records that failure.

📱 What about your phone or another computer?

The notice arrives wherever you choose. The form only opens on the computer where Zelador is installed. To open it on your phone or another computer, you need a private network, a secure connection between your devices. Your AI can guide you.

💻 Mac and Windows

We haven't tested them yet. Outside Linux, it would be able to see much less, so for now, don't count on it for these systems.

🔕 What if no message arrives?

If you turned on the notice, check what's going on. It sends a line even on quiet nights, so silence means something stopped the round. If that happens, ask your AI to check.

Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need to know how to program?

No. You paste the prompt, read the plan, and say yes or no. Your AI installs it.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Zelador is free. You only need to already have Claude Code or Codex. The AI explanation is optional and uses a little of their capacity.

How do I receive the notice?

You choose during installation. Telegram is best because the message waits for you in the morning: you need to create a bot there, and your AI can guide you. You can also use an on-screen notification, which appears overnight and might be missed, or just save the report.

What if I already keep track of everything?

It never overwrites your files. The initial check shows what it can use and what might need adjusting, and nothing changes until you say yes.

Does my data leave my computer?

Zelador doesn't have any internet service of its own. Only what you turn on leaves your computer, but keep in mind: the notice may include parts of the records, and the explanation sends the details of each item and the end of the records to your AI, with passwords hidden. If the records contain client or patient data, ask your AI to remove the excerpts from the notice and leave the explanation turned off.

Does it fix errors on its own?

No, by design. It gives you a suggestion, and you decide. Then you just ask your AI to do what you approved.
