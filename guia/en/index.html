In short

When you leave bots and AI tasks running on their own, you only find out about problems when someone complains. Zelador is a free program that keeps an eye on them for you: every night, it checks what failed on your computer, and every week it puts the issues worth fixing into a simple form for you to review. If you don’t keep track of what goes wrong yet, it creates the lists. If you already keep track, it uses your lists. To install it, just ask your AI.

How it works

A building caretaker, without the jargon

Think of a building caretaker: they make rounds at night, write down what they find, then ask the building manager what should be fixed.

🔦 1. The nighttime round

Every night, it reads what your computer recorded and separates what is serious from what just needs attention. Then it sends you a summary.

📋 2. The week’s agenda

Every Monday, it gathers recurring errors, bad nights, things that held you up, and forgotten tasks, with a priority for each one.

✍️ 3. Your decision

With your AI connected, each item explains what happened, why it matters, and what the suggestion would do. Without it, you get a ready-made summary. You decide what happens.

🧩 It adapts to what you have

Don’t keep track of anything yet? It creates four simple lists: what went wrong, what you learned, what held you up, and what’s still pending.

Already using different names for your lists? If you don’t have its lists yet, it uses yours without making copies, and your AI checks whether the format works.

Already have other alerts? It suggests using what they produce.

🔒 What it doesn’t do

It doesn’t fix anything on its own: it finds issues, explains them, and makes suggestions.

It doesn’t overwrite your files.

It doesn’t send your data to one of its services, because there isn’t one. Only what you enable leaves your computer: the notice and the explanation through your AI.

Before you install

Is it right for you?

It’s best to know now what it can and can’t see.

✅ It works for you if

  • Your tasks run on a computer with Linux, an operating system like Windows that is often used on computers that stay on all the time, and they were scheduled under the same user account you use to log in. Not sure? Your AI’s check will tell you before installation.
  • This computer usually stays on. If it’s off during the nighttime round, Zelador checks when it starts up again. If no one is logged in because the computer restarted or you signed out, it won’t run until a one-time change is made with the administrator password. Your AI’s initial check will tell you about this and whether anything is missing.
  • You use Claude Code or Codex, which can install it and check whether your computer has what it needs.

🚫 What it can’t see

  • What runs on internet services, such as a scheduled post on a social network.
  • Tasks run by the administrator or another computer user.
  • A scheduled task that doesn’t keep a log, a file where it records what it did. In that case, the notice says it couldn’t check.
  • A task that records an error but finishes as if it succeeded.
  • An error the task doesn’t write to the log, or describes in words Zelador doesn’t recognize. Ask your AI to check each task during installation.
  • Tasks that run in the cloud, even ones created by your AI.
  • A task that simply stopped running without recording an error.
  • A wrong result that finished without an error, unless that failure was recorded.

📱 What about your phone or another computer?

The notice arrives wherever you choose. The form only opens on the computer where Zelador is installed. To open it on your phone or another computer, you need a private network, a secure connection between your devices. Your AI can guide you.

💻 Mac and Windows

We haven’t tested them yet. Outside Linux, Zelador would see much less, so for now don’t count on it working on those systems.

🔕 What if no message arrives?

If you turned on notices, check that something hasn’t gone wrong. It sends a line even on a quiet night, so silence means something may have stopped the round. If that happens, ask your AI to check.

Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste the prompt, read the plan, and say yes or no. Your AI installs it.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Zelador is free. The AI explanation is optional and uses a small amount of the AI usage you already have.

How do I get the notice?

You choose during installation. Telegram is best: it waits for you to check in the morning. You need to create a bot there, and your AI will guide you. You can also use an on-screen notification, which appears overnight and is easy to miss, or just save the report.

What if I already keep track of everything?

It never overwrites your files. The initial check shows what it can use and what needs adjusting, and nothing changes until you say yes.

Does my data leave my computer?

Zelador doesn’t have an internet service. Only what you enable leaves your computer, but keep in mind: the notice may include parts of your logs, and the explanation sends the details of each item to your AI. If your logs contain customer or patient data, ask your AI to remove log excerpts from the notice and leave the explanation turned off.

Does it fix errors on its own?

No, by design. It gives you a suggestion, and you decide. Then just ask your AI to do what you approved.
