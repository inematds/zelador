In short

When you let bots and AI routines run on their own, you only find out about a problem when someone complains. Zelador is a free program that keeps an eye on things for you: every night, it checks what went wrong on your computer, and every week it gathers what is worth improving into a simple form for you to decide. If you don't keep notes about problems yet, it creates the lists. If you already keep notes, it uses yours. To install it, just ask your AI.

How it works

A building caretaker, without the jargon

Think of a building caretaker: they make their rounds at night, write down what they find, and then ask the building manager what needs fixing.

🔦 1. The night round

Every night, it reads what your computer recorded and sorts out what's serious and what just needs attention. After a few days, it learns the usual schedule for each task and notices when one stops. Then it sends the summary.

📋 2. The weekly list

Every Monday, it gathers recurring errors, bad nights, things that blocked you, and forgotten tasks, with a priority for each.

✍️ 3. Your decision

When your AI is connected, each item explains what happened, why it matters, and what the suggestion does. Without it, you get a ready-to-use summary. You decide.

🧩 It adapts to what you have

Don't keep notes yet? It creates four simple lists: what went wrong, what you learned, what blocked you, and what can wait.

Already keep notes under other names? If you don't have its lists yet, it uses yours without making copies, and your AI checks whether the format will work.

Already have other alerts? It suggests using what they produce.

🔒 What it doesn't do

It doesn't fix anything on its own: it finds problems, explains them, and makes suggestions.

It doesn't overwrite your files.

It doesn't send your data to a service of its own, because there isn't one. The only things that leave your computer are what you enable: the notice and the explanation through your AI.

Before you install

Is it right for you?

It's best to know now what it can and can't see.

✅ It works for you if

  • Your tasks run on a computer with Linux, an operating system like Windows that's often used on computers that stay on all the time, and were scheduled under the same user account you use to log in. Not sure? Your AI can check before installing.
  • The computer is usually on. If it's off during the night round, it makes the round when it turns back on. If no one is logged in because it restarted or you logged out, it won't run until a one-time change is made with the administrator password. Your AI's initial check will tell you this and whether anything is missing.
  • You use Claude Code or Codex. They can install it and check whether your computer has what it needs.

🚫 What it can't see

  • Tasks that run on internet services, such as a scheduled post inside a social network.
  • Tasks run by the computer's administrator or another user.
  • A scheduled task that doesn't keep a record, a file where it notes what it did. In that case, the notice says it couldn't check.
  • An error the task doesn't record, or records in words it doesn't recognize. Ask your AI to check each task during installation.
  • Tasks that run in the cloud, even ones created by your AI.
  • A wrong result that leaves no sign, unless someone writes down the problem.

📱 What about your phone or another computer?

You can choose where the notice arrives. The form only opens on the computer where Zelador is installed. To open it on your phone or another computer, you need a private network, a secure connection between your devices. Your AI can guide you.

💻 Mac and Windows

We haven't tested them yet. Outside Linux, it would see much less, so don't count on it working on those systems for now.

🔕 What if no message arrives?

If you turned on notices, check it out. It sends a line even on quiet nights, so silence means something may have stopped the round. If that happens, ask your AI to check.

Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste the request, read the plan, and say yes or no. Your AI does the installation.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Zelador is free. The AI explanation is optional and uses a little of the AI access you already have.

How do I get the notice?

You choose during installation. Telegram is best because the notice waits for you in the morning: you need to create a bot there, and your AI can guide you. You can also use an on-screen notification, which appears overnight and is easy to miss, or just save the report.

What if I already keep notes about everything?

It never overwrites your files. The initial check shows what it can use and what would need adjusting, and nothing changes until you say yes.

Does my data leave my computer?

Zelador has no internet service of its own. Only what you enable leaves your computer, but keep in mind: the notice may include excerpts from your records, and the explanation sends your AI the details of each item. If your records contain client or patient data, ask your AI to remove the excerpts from the notice and leave the explanation turned off.

Does it fix errors on its own?

No, by design. It gives you a suggestion, and you decide. Then just ask your AI to do what you approved.
